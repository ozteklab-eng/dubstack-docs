---

Frames give images and visual examples consistent spacing, borders, and optional captions.

<Frame caption="Use captions to explain what the reader is seeing.">
  <img src="/logo/light.svg" alt="DubStack logo" />
</Frame>

```mdx
<Frame caption="Use captions to explain what the reader is seeing.">
  <img src="/logo/light.svg" alt="DubStack logo" />
</Frame>
```

## When to use frames

- Product screenshots.
- Diagrams.
- Short visual examples.
- Media that needs a caption.

## Supported props

<ResponseField name="caption" type="string">
  Optional text displayed below the framed content.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="children" type="HTML or MDX content" required>
  The visual content inside the frame. Images are resolved through the tenant asset pipeline.
</ResponseField>
