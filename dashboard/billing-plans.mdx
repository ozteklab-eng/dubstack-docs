Billing and plan settings show the workspace's current subscription state and enabled features.

Use this page to understand how plan state affects product behavior.

## Plans

The dashboard is the source of truth for current plan names, prices, allowances, and trial state.

Common workspace states include:

| Plan state | Editor seats | AI credits | Current public pricing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 1 | No permanent allowance outside an active trial or entitlement. | Included. |
| Pro | Unlimited | 10,000 per month. | USD 199/month, or USD 1,908/year when billed yearly. |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | A committed monthly allowance defined in the agreement. | Custom. |

Pro workspaces can be billed monthly or annually when both options are available for the workspace.

Pro and Enterprise are organization subscriptions rather than per-seat subscriptions. Editor seats are not multiplied into the Stripe quantity. Product limits and AI usage are controlled through organization entitlements and the monthly AI credit allowance shown in the dashboard.

<Callout type="info">
  Annual Pro billing is displayed as USD 159/month in the dashboard and is charged as USD 1,908/year.
</Callout>

## What billing controls

Billing can affect:

- custom domains
- team size
- Theme Studio access
- SSO and enterprise controls
- assistant and agent usage
- workflow and integration availability
- support level

The dashboard is the source of truth for your current plan, trial state, and available upgrade paths.

For assistant, agent, Slack, and workflow usage details, see [Usage](/dashboard/usage) and [AI credits and usage](/ai/credits-usage).

## Upgrade to Pro

Organization admins can open **Settings > Organization > Billing** to review Pro and start self-service checkout.

The Pro upgrade flow uses a hosted Stripe Checkout page. After Stripe confirms checkout, dubstack updates the organization subscription state and refreshes feature entitlements.

Checkout can collect billing details, tax information, and payment method details when required for the workspace and billing country. Do not share card details, tax identifiers, or billing secrets with support unless support explicitly asks you to use a secure channel.

Use the billing portal from the same page to:

- update payment method
- view invoices and receipts
- update billing details
- manage tax information when available
- cancel or change the subscription when the workspace is eligible

If the dashboard still shows the old plan after checkout, refresh the billing page. If the state does not update, contact support with the workspace slug and checkout time.

## Start an Enterprise agreement

Enterprise is sales-assisted and cannot be enabled from customer billing settings.

1. Select **Contact sales** from the Enterprise plan.
2. Share your organization, rollout requirements, desired billing interval, expected AI usage, security requirements, and support needs.
3. Agree on the recurring USD amount, committed monthly AI credits, and service terms.
4. A DubStack operator issues an organization-scoped Stripe Checkout link.
5. Complete checkout using the billing contact named in the agreement.
6. Wait for Stripe confirmation. Enterprise features are not enabled from the success-page redirect alone.

The Enterprise checkout contains one organization subscription with quantity `1`. Unlimited editor seats do not create separate Stripe line items. The signed Stripe webhook activates the Enterprise tier and its agreed monthly AI credit entitlement together.

<Callout type="warning">
  A quote or checkout link does not grant Enterprise access. The organization remains on its existing plan until Stripe confirms the subscription through the billing webhook.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Pro checkout and subscription management are dashboard-only admin actions. Enterprise checkout is issued by DubStack Ops after agreement and is not part of the public docs runtime API.
</Callout>

## Taxes and invoices

Tax collection can depend on your billing country, business status, and tax details.

When tax collection is enabled for checkout, enter accurate billing details before completing payment. Business customers should provide the correct company name and tax identifier where applicable.

Invoices and receipts are available from the billing portal after a successful payment.

## Trials

New workspaces can start with a 14-day Pro trial. Trials can temporarily enable higher-tier features. During a trial, validate the features your team actually needs:

- custom domain setup
- team access
- agent workflows
- assistant behavior
- advanced branding or Theme Studio

Before the trial ends, decide whether the workspace should upgrade or reduce usage to the current plan.

## Feature unavailable

If a feature is locked:

1. Check the plan badge or lock message.
2. Confirm you are in the right workspace.
3. Confirm the feature is not disabled by integration state.
4. Open billing settings to review upgrade options.

## Billing safety

Only trusted workspace admins should manage billing, plan changes, and cancellation actions.

Plan changes can affect public docs behavior when a feature depends on an entitlement.

Editors and viewers should not need billing access for normal documentation work. See [Roles](/dashboard/roles) for the permissions model.
